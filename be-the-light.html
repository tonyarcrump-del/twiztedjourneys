If you are in crisis or need immediate help:
Call or text 988 — the Suicide & Crisis Lifeline — free, 24/7, confidential.
Veterans, service members & military families: dial 988 and press 1, or text 838255 (Veterans Crisis Line).
Visit 988lifeline.org  |  Crisis Text Line: text HOME to 741741  |  Visit crisistextline.org
