Community Stories

"Its ok if today the only thing you accomplished was to breathe."

At Twizted Journeys we believe that storytelling is healing. Each story contributes to breaking the stigma surrounding mental illness, grief, and addiction. Whether you're continuing after your own pause or someone else's pause, your words can offer comfort, courage, and connection. Sharing your continue could be just the light someone in the darkness needs.

Share Your Journey
Content Disclosure

Twizted Journeys shares personal stories for awareness, connection, and hope. Some stories may include grief, suicide loss, addiction, overdose, trauma, or mental health struggles. These stories are not therapy, medical advice, crisis counseling, or emergency support. If you or someone you know is in immediate danger, call emergency services now. In the United States, call or text 988 or visit 988lifeline.org for crisis support.

If you are under 18: Please have a parent or guardian help you before submitting a story or sharing personal details publicly. We want your voice heard safely.
