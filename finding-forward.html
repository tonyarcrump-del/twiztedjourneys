Finding Forward

The Virtual Journey

Finding Forward is brought to you by Twizted Journeys. This peer support group meets weekly through Zoom. If you find yourself on a Twizted journey as a suicide loss survivor, please join us. Our program brings people together through connection, remembrance, understanding, and support. If you would like to know more, please click the link below.

What to Expect

Support, Connection, Tears, and Laughter

Finding Forward is not about having the perfect words. It is a virtual space for connection, listening, remembering, and finding forward together with care.

  • Open to people continuing after the pause left by suicide loss or unexpected death.
  • Community-centered support with Twizted Journeys’ existing tone of compassion, honesty, remembrance, and hope.
  • No clinical claims, diagnosis, treatment, or therapy promises.

If you or someone you know is in immediate danger, call emergency services now.
If you are in the United States and experiencing thoughts of suicide, emotional distress, or a mental health crisis, call or text 988 or visit 988lifeline.org Veterans and service members: press 1 for the Veterans Crisis Line..

Finding Forward and Twizted Journeys are not emergency or crisis-response services.
