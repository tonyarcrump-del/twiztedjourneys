Finding Forward

The Virtual Journey

Finding Forward is a recurring virtual Twizted Journeys program for people looking for connection, remembrance, support, and a place to keep moving after hard seasons.

What to Expect

Supportive, Low-Barrier, Human

Finding Forward is not about having the perfect words. It is a virtual space for connection, listening, remembering, and continuing forward together with care.

  • Open to people carrying grief, suicide loss, emotional stress, mental health struggles, or the journey of someone they love.
  • Community-centered support with Twizted Journeys’ existing tone of compassion, honesty, remembrance, and hope.
  • No clinical claims, diagnosis, treatment, or therapy promises.

Important: Finding Forward is not an emergency response service, therapy, crisis counseling, medical care, or a substitute for professional support.
